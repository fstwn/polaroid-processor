    Place: drag the rectangle to move it, its corners or sides to resize it, the round handle above the top edge (or the Rotation field) to turn it, or drag on empty area to draw a new one. With 600 or Go it keeps the proportions of that print. Fine-tune then moves each corner on its own. Save works from both steps.
    Drag a magenta corner or the green grip on an edge tile onto the print edge. With a photo crop on, the cyan line is the photo window: switch to Photo window and drag its corners onto the picture's edge. Click a tile to enlarge it: arrow keys move 1 px, Shift + arrow 10 px, Esc closes.
